About me

Computer Science student, web developer and hardware tinkerer.

My story

I'm Muneeb Ur Rehman Sidhar, a Computer Science student at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Taxila. I like understanding how things work, from the page on the screen all the way down to the circuit underneath.

On the web I build both sides of an application: the front end with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and the back end with Node.js. In C++ and Python I focus on clean, object-oriented design and on solving problems one step at a time.

Away from the browser I work on Arduino and Digital Logic Design (DLD) projects, where code meets real components. That mix of software and hardware is what I enjoy most.

What drives me

I want to become a software engineer who can work across the whole stack and also understand the hardware underneath. Every project is a chance to learn a new tool, so I keep building, breaking and improving. I am also learning AI and cyber security, and I have completed three Coursera courses in these areas.

I am looking for job offers, internships and freelance projects where I can contribute from day one and keep growing with a good team.
